## STUDY TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### **DO:**
- ✅ Stick to the daily schedule (consistency > intensity)
- ✅ Time yourself on practice exams
- ✅ Review explanations, not just answers
- ✅ Track your weak areas and revisit them
- ✅ Read questions carefully (avoid silly mistakes)
- ✅ Sleep well the night before the certification
- ✅ Celebrate small wins each day

### **DON'T:**
- ❌ Cram on Day 31 (mental prep, not learning)
- ❌ Skip explaining wrong answers (that's where learning happens)
- ❌ Memorize answers (understand concepts instead)
- ❌ Worry if Day 1 scores are low (you'll improve)
- ❌ Change your study method mid-way (trust the process)
- ❌ Rush through questions (quality > speed)

## QUICK REFERENCE: PASSING STRATEGIES

| Challenge | Strategy |
|-----------|----------|
| Running out of time | Practice with a timer daily; aim for 1 min/question |
| Forgetting concepts | Review notes from weak days before each mock exam |
| Similar wrong answers | Identify the misconception and create a memorable counter-example |
| Careless mistakes | Read questions twice; flag keywords before answering |
| Confidence issues | Focus on improvement, not perfection. You'll improve 20+ points over 32 days |
| Burnout | Take 1-2 mini-breaks during each study session (5 min walk, water, stretch) |

## CONTINGENCY: FALLING BEHIND?

If you miss a day or fall behind:
- **1-2 days behind:** Combine two days' content into 75 min sessions (move non-critical reviews)
- **3-4 days behind:** Skip domain reviews on Days 15 (catch up over Days 23-27)
- **5+ days behind:** Adjust schedule to focus on mock exams (they're highest ROI for your time)
- **Weak on a specific domain:** Spend Day 28-29 drilling that domain's questions intensively
